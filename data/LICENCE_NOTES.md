# ListenBrainz data licence

- **Licence:** CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). ListenBrainz states that user listen
  data is made public under CC0 and that users agree to have their listening history
  included in public dumps when they sign up (private profile data is excluded).
- **Attribution:** not legally required under CC0. We credit it anyway:
  "Listening data from ListenBrainz (https://listenbrainz.org), a MetaBrainz Foundation
  project, released under CC0."
- **Limits:** none from the licence. Our own rules still apply: hash user names on
  ingest, publish aggregates only, no per-user profiles.
- **Dump used:** listenbrainz-dump-2758-20261101-000003-full (1 Nov 2026), spark format.
- Sources: https://listenbrainz.readthedocs.io/en/latest/users/listenbrainz-dumps.html ,
  https://listenbrainz.org/data/
- Checked: 2026-11-03.
